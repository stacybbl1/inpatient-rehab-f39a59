Who we are

Welcome to Ethan Crossing Addiction Treatment Center of Cleveland, where individuals struggling with substance use disorders find the structured, immersive care that real recovery demands. Situated at 20611 Euclid Ave in Euclid, Ohio, the center provides residential inpatient treatment that removes clients from the environments and triggers that fuel active addiction. With a compassionate clinical team and evidence-based programming, Ethan Crossing delivers the kind of focused, around-the-clock support that gives people the best possible foundation for lasting sobriety.
